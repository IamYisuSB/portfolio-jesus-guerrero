Cookies and similar technologies, such as the browser's local storage (localStorage), are small files or pieces of data that a website stores on your device. This English version is provided for information only; the Spanish version prevails.

1. What this site uses

This site uses no analytics, advertising or social media cookies and loads no third-party content: fonts are served from its own domain. It only stores the following technical, essential data in your browser:

NameTypePurposeDuration
themeFirst-party · localStorageRemember whether you prefer light or dark mode.Until you delete it
